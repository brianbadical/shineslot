Badical HQ privacy policy

Badical HQ is a private tool run by Brian Smith for his own small businesses (including ShineSlot, Save Point Siren and Lanternhush). It is not offered to the public.

What it uses Google sign-in for: uploading and reading stats for Brian's own YouTube channels, and sending and reading email from Brian's own Gmail account.

What it keeps: the sign-in token is stored only on Brian's own computer. No Google data is sold, shared with others, or used for ads.

Removing access: you can remove Badical HQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
