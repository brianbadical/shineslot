Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Snag Goblin website and the Snag Goblin Poster app.

What the app uses

The Snag Goblin Poster app connects only to the owner's own TikTok account, after the owner signs in and allows it. It uses basic profile info (to confirm the right account is connected) and permission to upload videos as drafts. It does not read messages, followers, or anyone else's data.

Where it is kept

The sign-in key TikTok gives the app is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.

Visitors

This website does not use cookies, ads, or tracking. Shopping links go to Amazon, which has its own privacy policy.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.
