Rather do it yourself?

  1. Go to business.google.com on your phone and search your business name. Claim it, or add it if it's not there.
  2. Use your real business name only. No extra words like "best" or your city.
  3. If you work at customers' homes, choose "I deliver to customers" and hide your home address.
  4. Google will ask for a short video: show your truck or sign, your tools, and a business paper.
  5. Add hours, what you do, the towns you serve, 5+ photos of your work, and your booking link.
  6. Ask every customer for a review after each job.
